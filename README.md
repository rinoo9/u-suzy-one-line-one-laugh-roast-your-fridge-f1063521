# One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521) bounty "One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge".

`main.py` writes one roast line about the entrant's fridge, under
200 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My fridge hums all night like it's warming up for a concert, but the only thing it has ever kept fresh is the smell of last week's leftovers.

Why it works: the hum is the most recognisable thing about a fridge —
everyone has heard it at 2 a.m. Turning that drone into a warm-up act
gives the appliance delusions of grandeur, and the punchline lands on
the twist: the one thing this tireless performer actually preserves
isn't the food, it's the smell of the food it failed to save.
